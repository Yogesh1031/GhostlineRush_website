How it works

Simple to learn. Impossible to master.

👻

Ghost Echo mechanic

Every 8 seconds, your path becomes a ghost that loops forever. The longer you survive, the more ghosts fill the screen.

🚀

Constant movement

Your ship never stops. Swipe to change direction — but choose wisely. Every turn shapes your next ghost.

💎

Collect orbs

Navigate through orbs to earn currency. Use orbs to unlock new universes, ships, and power-ups.

⚡

200 levels

Speed increases, obstacles appear, and ghost behaviour evolves. Each universe brings entirely new challenges.

👥

Multiplayer (coming soon)

Your ghosts haunt opponents. Play with friends or compete globally. Last ship standing wins.

🏆

Global leaderboard

Compete with players worldwide. Weekly seasons reset rankings and give top players exclusive rewards.
