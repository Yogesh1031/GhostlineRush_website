Summary: Ghostline Rush is a free mobile game. We collect minimal data necessary to run the game. We do not sell your personal information to anyone. You can play as a guest without creating an account.

1. Who We Are

Ghostline Rush is developed and operated by Yogesh Goyal ("we", "us", "our"). Our website is ghostlinerush.com. For any privacy questions, contact us at support@ghostlinerush.com.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information you provide

  • Guest login: We create an anonymous user ID automatically. No name, email, or personal details required.
  • Facebook login (optional): If you choose to log in with Facebook, we receive your Facebook name and profile picture. We do not receive your email or any other Facebook data.

2.2 Information collected automatically

  • Game progress — levels completed, score, orbs earned
  • Device type and operating system version
  • App version
  • General location (country level only — used for ad targeting)
  • Crash reports and error logs

2.3 Information collected by third-party SDKs

Our game uses third-party services that may collect data independently:

  • Appodeal (Advertising): Collects device identifiers and usage data for ad serving. See Appodeal's Privacy Policy.
  • Google Play Services: Collects data per Google's Privacy Policy for billing and distribution.
  • Facebook SDK: If you use Facebook login. See Facebook's Privacy Policy.
  • Supabase: Our backend database provider. Data is stored on servers in the Mumbai, India region. See Supabase's Privacy Policy.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To save and sync your game progress across devices
  • To display advertisements (rewarded videos, interstitial ads)
  • To process in-app purchases
  • To maintain global leaderboards
  • To fix bugs and improve the game
  • To prevent cheating and ensure fair play

4. In-App Purchases

All in-app purchases on the Android app will be processed through Google Play Billing once the app is live. We do not store your payment card details. Google handles all payment processing. For website purchases, payments are processed through Cashfree Payments.

5. Advertising

Ghostline Rush shows advertisements through Appodeal's mediation platform. Ads may be personalised based on your general location and device type. You can opt out of personalised ads through your device settings:

  • Android: Settings → Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalisation
  • iOS: Settings → Privacy → Tracking → Allow Apps to Request to Track (OFF)

6. Data Storage and Security

Your game data is stored on Supabase servers located in Mumbai, India. We use industry-standard encryption for data in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and at rest. We implement Row Level Security so each player can only access their own data.

7. Children's Privacy

Ghostline Rush is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us personal information, please contact us at support@ghostlinerush.com and we will delete it immediately.

8. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your account and all associated data
  • Export your data in a portable format

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at support@ghostlinerush.com. We will respond within 30 days.

9. Account Deletion

To delete your account and all associated data, either:

  • Go to Settings in the game → Account → Delete Account
  • Email us at support@ghostlinerush.com with subject "Delete My Account"

All data will be permanently deleted within 7 days of your request.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes through an in-game notification. Continued use of the game after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

11. Contact Us

For any privacy-related questions or requests:

  • Email: support@ghostlinerush.com
  • Website: ghostlinerush.com/support
